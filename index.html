      How Coast ranks routes

      Coast asks OSRM for up to three road alternatives, samples each path against a terrain elevation model, and runs a simple physics estimate: rolling resistance and aerodynamic drag always cost energy, climbs cost extra, and descents return a share of their potential energy through regenerative braking at your regen efficiency setting.

      Routes are ranked by estimated net battery use, which naturally favors downhill-heavy paths where braking energy is recovered instead of lost. Physics still applies: a detour that climbs first can never pay for itself, so the winner is the route that wastes the least. All figures are estimates for comparison, not range predictions.
